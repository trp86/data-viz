PRODUCTION NOTES
====================================================================================================

Music Suggestions:
- Scene 1: Dramatic, tension-building (low strings, subtle percussion)
- Scene 2-4: Informative, steady (ambient electronic, mid-tempo)
- Scene 5: Contrasting (hopeful for success, somber for struggles)
- Scene 6: Uplifting resolution (building to positive close)

Voiceover Style:
- Professional, authoritative but accessible
- Clear enunciation, moderate pace (~150 words/minute)
- Emotional inflection on key statistics
- Pause for visual impact on big numbers

Transitions:
- Smooth crossfades (0.5-1 second)
- Data-driven wipes (numbers morphing)
- Map zoom transitions between scenes
- Minimal hard cuts

Color Psychology:
- Red/Orange: Urgency, heat, concentration
- Yellow/Gold: Hope, improvement, attention
- Green: Success, positive change
- Dark background: Focus, seriousness, premium feel

Typography:
- Titles: Bold, sans-serif, 48-72pt
- Body text: Clean, readable, 24-36pt
- Numbers: Large, impactful, 60-120pt
- Source: Small, unobtrusive, 16-20pt

Pacing:
- Fast hook (grab attention in 5 seconds)
- Moderate body (allow data absorption)
- Strong close (memorable takeaway)
